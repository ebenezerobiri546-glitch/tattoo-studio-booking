SESSION PREPARATION & POLICIES

Essential guidelines before your scheduled needle session

Hydration & Meals

Eat a substantial carb-rich meal 1–2 hours prior to arrival and drink plenty of water to maintain blood sugar.

No Alcohol or Blood Thinners

Avoid alcohol, aspirin, and heavy caffeine 24 hours prior to your session to prevent excessive bleeding.

Non-Refundable Deposits

Deposits lock your calendar date and stencil preparation time. Deducted in full from the final tattoo total.
